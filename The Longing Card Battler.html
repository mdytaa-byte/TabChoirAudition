Chronicle

Battlefield Keywords

Guard must be attacked first. Swift may attack when summoned. Flight ignores Guard. Ward prevents the first damage. Drain heals your commander when dealing damage. Rebirth returns once with 1 Health. A board holds at most five units.

How to Play

Each commander begins with 30 Health and a faction deck. Your maximum Essence rises by one at the start of each turn, up to ten, and refills completely.

1. DeployPlay units, spells, and one persistent relic by paying their Essence cost.
2. CommandSelect a ready friendly unit, then an enemy unit or commander. Most newly summoned units must wait one turn.
3. DefendUnits strike each other simultaneously. Guard units protect allies and commanders unless the attacker has Flight.
4. WinReduce the opposing commander to 0 Health. Running out of cards causes escalating fatigue damage.
Local PlayA pass screen hides each player’s hand between turns.
