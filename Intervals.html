Intervals Fundamentals

1. What Is an Interval?

An interval is the distance between two pitches.

  • Small distances create small intervals.
  • Larger distances create larger intervals.
  • Intervals can be heard and seen in notation.

2. Melodic vs. Harmonic Intervals

A melodic interval is played one note after another.

A harmonic interval is played at the same time.

3. Interval Numbers

Interval numbers are determined by counting letter names.

Example Interval
C–D 2nd
C–E 3rd
C–F 4th
C–G 5th
C–A 6th
C–B 7th
C–C Octave

4. Interval Quality

Qualities describe the exact size of intervals.

  • Perfect
  • Major
  • Minor
  • Augmented
  • Diminished

Examples:

  • C–E = Major 3rd
  • C–E♭ = Minor 3rd
  • C–F = Perfect 4th
  • C–F♯ = Augmented 4th
  • C–G♭ = Diminished 5th
