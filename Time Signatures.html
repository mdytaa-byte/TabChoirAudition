Time Signatures

What Does a Time Signature Mean?

4
4

The top number tells how many beats are in each measure.

The bottom number tells which note value receives one beat.

  • 2 = Half Note
  • 4 = Quarter Note
  • 8 = Eighth Note

Common Time Signatures

Time Signature Meaning
2/4 2 quarter-note beats
3/4 3 quarter-note beats
4/4 4 quarter-note beats
6/8 2 groups of 3 eighth notes
9/8 3 groups of 3 eighth notes
12/8 4 groups of 3 eighth notes

Simple vs Compound Meter

Simple meter divides each beat into 2 parts.

  • 2/4
  • 3/4
  • 4/4

Compound meter divides each beat into 3 parts.

  • 6/8
  • 9/8
  • 12/8
