Pitch Notation Fundamentals

Learn

  • Staff: Five lines and four spaces used to show pitch.
  • Clefs: Treble and Bass clefs tell us which pitches the staff represents.
  • Letter Names: A B C D E F G repeat continuously.
  • Ledger Lines: Short lines above and below the staff.
  • Accidentals: ♯ sharp, ♭ flat, ♮ natural.
  • Enharmonic Notes: C♯ and D♭ sound the same but are written differently.
  • Octaves: Notes with the same letter name at different pitch levels.
