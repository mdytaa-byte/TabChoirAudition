Major and Minor Scales

What Is a Scale?

A scale is an organized collection of pitches arranged from low to high.

The first note is called the tonic or keynote.

Modes

Modes are scales built from different starting points within a scale.

  • Ionian = Major Scale
  • Aeolian = Natural Minor Scale
  • Dorian
  • Phrygian
  • Lydian
  • Mixolydian
  • Locrian

For now, remember:

  • Ionian = Major
  • Aeolian = Minor
