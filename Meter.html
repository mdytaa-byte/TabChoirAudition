Meter Fundamentals

What Is Meter?

Meter is the way beats are grouped in music.

A beat is the steady pulse that we tap our foot to.

Meter organizes beats into recurring groups.

  • Duple = groups of 2 beats
  • Triple = groups of 3 beats
  • Quadruple = groups of 4 beats

Simple vs. Compound Meter

In simple meter, each beat divides into two parts.

In compound meter, each beat divides into three parts.

Simple Compound
2/4 6/8
3/4 9/8
4/4 12/8
